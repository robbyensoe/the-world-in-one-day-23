# Sources — the stories on the page

All stories happened, or were first reported, on Monday 23 November 2026.

- **Global markets / Nvidia** — Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang
  as CEO, president and board member (announced Monday morning, after a
  unanimous board vote on 22 Nov), and the shares finished up 31.48% at
  $165.05 — the stock's best day since 2003 — adding about $950 billion
  in market value. The S&P 500 rose 4.73%, the Nasdaq Composite 6.51%
  and the Dow 1,448 points (Reuters, 23 Nov; CNN, 23 Nov; WSJ, 23 Nov;
  Le Monde, 23 Nov; Investopedia, 23 Nov). President Trump, who had
  pressed for Huang's return, made up to $15m on his Nvidia stock, and
  Senator Ruben Gallego called for an investigation into stock positions
  taken before the announcement (The Brief, 23 Nov).
- **Libya / Ukraine** — President Zelensky confirmed that Ukrainian sea
  drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury in Benghazi port; a Russian
  cargo freighter was also attacked by drones in the Baltic the same
  day (Principia, 23 Nov; Broadcast Prism, 23 Nov; Breitbart, 23 Nov).
- **Indonesia** — a fire at a sweets factory in Cikande, Banten, killed
  at least 24 people, with about 30 workers still missing as rescuers
  searched the ruins (Principia, 23 Nov). The confirmed toll rose to 26
  the following day.
- **Space / Mercury** — the ESA–JAXA BepiColombo spacecraft entered
  orbit around Mercury after an eight-year journey (Principia,
  23 Nov).
- **Climate / COP31** — the UN climate summit in Turkey, the longest on
  record, ended on 23 November with no agreement, as countries split
  over fossil-fuel phase-out language and adaptation finance
  (Principia, 23 Nov; Broadcast Prism, 23 Nov). Sunday's presidency
  draft had already omitted fossil fuels; Monday the talks closed
  without approving a deal.
- **Ukraine / Kyiv** — Russian bombardment of the Kyiv region killed at
  least 15 people; a boy was pulled alive from the rubble (Principia,
  23 Nov).
- **UK / EU** — Prime Minister Andy Burnham defended the new EU reset
  agreement in the Commons, citing a claimed £17bn annual benefit by
  2040 and promising no rejoin without a referendum (Broadcast Prism,
  23 Nov).
- **AI / mathematics** — Anthropic said its AI had helped prove the
  Riemann hypothesis, prompting debate among mathematicians over
  whether a machine-generated proof can be understood or verified
  (Principia, 23 Nov).
- **Sport** — FIFA confirmed the United Kingdom — England, Scotland,
  Wales and Northern Ireland — will host the 2035 Women's World Cup
  (Broadcast Prism, 23 Nov); Manchester's Co-op Live arena was
  evacuated during a Don Toliver concert after an unknown substance
  was thrown (Broadcast Prism, 23 Nov).
- **In brief** — drone strikes hit homes and a United Nations building
  in Khartoum, Sudan (Principia, 23 Nov); a suspected Russian drone
  crossed into Polish airspace for eight minutes during strikes on
  western Ukraine (The Brief, 23 Nov); the Trump administration
  unveiled sweeping new sanctions aimed at Iran's "last financial
  lifeline" (Breitbart, 23 Nov); euro-area inflation rose to 4.0%
  (Principia, 23 Nov); the FBI probed a possible hack of a crime
  database (Principia, 23 Nov).
